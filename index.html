Pradeep Angiras — Founder, Angiras & Co.™
Pradeep grew up in the mighty and fragile Himalayas, where the effects of a changing climate were visible early. A civil and construction-management professional with an MBA and a PMP, he specialised in green building after seeing the net-zero ambitions of cities and provinces across Canada — and approaches the work with attention to people, context, and the realities of getting things built.
